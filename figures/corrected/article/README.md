# Corrected article figures

The 16 figures preserve the article layout. Figures 5–8 use matched phase EOFs; Figures 9–16 use the validated corrected total EOF, extreme selection and four-cluster analysis. The latter are regenerated by `python -m scripts.article_figures.reproduce_downstream` after its legacy gate passes. Density color bars in Figures 9, 10 and 13 end at the observed panel maximum rounded to two decimals and retain their interval count.

Numerical results, both figure manifests and output hashes are in `results/corrected/article/`. The gate evidence is in `results/original/article/reproduction_gate/`. Paired images are in `figures/comparison/article/`.
