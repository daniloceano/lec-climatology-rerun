# Figures

## `original/article/`

The 16 PNGs referenced by the final revision-2 article submission. They are
copied verbatim from the legacy manuscript directory and are never overwritten
by corrected outputs.

## `corrected/article/`

Corrected-only reusable artifacts. Figures 1 and 2 are present because they do
not depend on corrected LEC values. Figures 3–16 use corrected data with the
same dimensions and panel composition as the published article. Each figure is
available as PNG and PDF. Figures 9–16 are generated by
`scripts/article_figures/reproduce_downstream.py` after the legacy gate passes.

## `comparison/article/`

The 16 PNG/PDF article outputs shown in the current
`docs/comparison/article_before_after_report.md`:

- Figures 1–8 retain their earlier validated comparison images;
- Figures 9–16 show the published image on the left and the validated corrected image on the right.

These figures compare the full archived article population with the validated
corrected rerun. Generate Figures 9–16 with
`scripts/article_figures/build_validated_comparison.py`.

## `comparison/paired_control/`

Technical diagnostics on the same 3,820 cyclones before and after correction.
They support the paired-control report and are not paper figures.

The article-layout corrected suite preserves the publication's four-cluster
definition. The older PDF report from the five-group comparison is historical;
the Markdown report above points to the current figures.
